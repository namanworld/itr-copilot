# Income Tax Department help page: relevant extracts

- **Scope:** Selected facts relevant to FY 2025-26 / AY 2026-27; this is an excerpt, not a full-page copy.

## Individual rates and surcharge

For individuals below age 60, the page lists the old-regime slab rates as 0% up to ₹2.5 lakh, 5% from ₹2.5–5 lakh, 20% from ₹5–10 lakh, and 30% above ₹10 lakh. It lists the new-regime slabs as 0% up to ₹4 lakh, followed by 5%, 10%, 15%, 20%, 25%, and 30% across successive ₹4 lakh bands, with the 30% rate above ₹24 lakh.

The surcharge table gives these rates for new regime / old regime respectively: 10% / 10% above ₹50 lakh, 15% / 15% above ₹1 crore, 25% / 25% above ₹2 crore, and 25% / 37% above ₹5 crore. The page states that health and education cess is 4% of income tax plus surcharge, where applicable, and describes surcharge marginal relief.

## Section 87A rebate

The page lists a maximum rebate of ₹60,000 for a resident individual under the new regime when taxable income does not exceed ₹12 lakh, and ₹12,500 under the old regime when taxable income does not exceed ₹5 lakh.

## Section 80D

For self, spouse, or dependent children, the page lists a ₹25,000 limit, or ₹50,000 if a person is a senior citizen. It gives a ₹5,000 preventive-health-check-up limit within that limit. It lists the same regular and senior-citizen limits for parents, with a separate ₹5,000 preventive-check-up limit included within the parents' limit.

## Section 24(b)

The page describes a maximum ₹2 lakh interest deduction for a self-occupied property in the applicable loan circumstances.

This extract records what the department page says; it does not independently validate that every modeled rule is applicable to every taxpayer.
