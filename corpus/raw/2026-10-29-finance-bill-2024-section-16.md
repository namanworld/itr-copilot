# Finance Bill 2024: section 16(ia) excerpt

- **Source PDF:** https://www.indiabudget.gov.in/budget2024-25/doc/Finance_Bill.pdf
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-29
- **Location:** Clause 10, page 39; explanatory note, page 168
- **Act amended:** Income-tax Act, 1961

Clause 10 inserts a proviso to section 16(ia): where tax is computed under section 115BAC(1A), the words “fifty thousand rupees” are to be read as “seventy-five thousand rupees.” The clause says this takes effect from 1-Apr-2025. The explanatory note confirms that the existing standard deduction is ₹50,000 and that the increase to ₹75,000 applies to assessment year 2025-26 and subsequent years.

This is a short excerpt of the official bill, not a copy of the complete PDF.
