# Finance Bill 2025: section 87A excerpt

- **Source PDF:** https://www.indiabudget.gov.in/budget2025-26/doc/Finance_Bill.pdf
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-29
- **Location:** Clause 20, page 43; explanatory note, page 149
- **Act amended:** Income-tax Act, 1961

Clause 20, effective 1-Apr-2026, substitutes ₹12 lakh for ₹7 lakh and ₹60,000 for ₹25,000 in the new-regime rebate provision. It also retains the provision that rebate cannot exceed income tax payable under section 115BAC(1A). The clause's explanatory note describes marginal relief for income above the threshold and states that the amendment extends that relief by replacing the ₹7 lakh threshold with ₹12 lakh.

The Income Tax Department help page retrieved on the same date lists the resulting ₹12 lakh threshold and ₹60,000 maximum rebate for AY 2026-27. This is a short excerpt of the official bill, not a copy of the complete PDF.
