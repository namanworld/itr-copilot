## Subsection (2): relevant deduction restrictions

For computing total income under subsection (1A), clause (i) generally disallows specified exemptions and deductions, including:

- the section 10(13A) house-rent allowance exemption and specified exemptions under other parts of section 10;
- specified deductions under sections 16, 24(b) for a property covered by section 23(2), and several business-related provisions;
- deductions under Chapter VI-A, except employer contributions under section 80CCD(2), contributions under section 80CCH(2), and section 80JJAA.

The page also presents clauses (ii)–(iv) of subsection (2). In summary, they restrict set-off of specified carried-forward losses and house-property losses, allow depreciation as prescribed except for additional depreciation under section 32(1)(iia), and disallow allowances or perquisites granted under other laws.

The page distinguishes the wording applicable to subsection (1A) from the earlier wording for subsection (1), and includes amendment notes. Check the applicable assessment-year text and statutory amendments before relying on this summary for a tax decision.
